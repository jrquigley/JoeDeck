Playlists

A playlist is a slideshow of images and websites. Set how long each item stays up. A playlist with a single item shows it indefinitely. The default playlist plays whenever no schedule rule is active.

Schedule

Rules switch to a different playlist on certain days and times. Rules are checked top to bottom; the first one that matches wins. A window like 22:00 → 06:00 runs overnight; 00:00 → 00:00 means all day. Uses this sign’s clock ().

Groups

A group makes several signs show the same content and change items at the same moment, for example every concourse TV showing the same promo loop. A sign can be in one group at a time. Turn a group off, or take a sign out of it, and the sign goes back to its own content.

Activity log

Who changed what, and when, across all the signs you can access. Each sign keeps its own history (newest 5,000 events), so signs that are switched off can't show theirs until they're back.
